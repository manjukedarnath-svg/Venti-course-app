# CLAUDE.md: VentCourse

This is a **live, working app**. Continue it; don't rebuild or migrate it.

## How the files work
- `index.html` holds the whole app. The top part is the template (HTML with `{{ name }}` holes, `<sc-if>`, `<sc-for>`, `<x-import>`). The bottom `<script type="text/x-dc">` is the logic: `class Component extends DCLogic { renderVals() {…} }`.
- Holes are plain names only. Compute values in `renderVals()` and expose them by name. No `{{ a + b }}`.
- Styling is **inline `style="…"` only**. No new CSS classes or stylesheets.
- Lesson, case and practice data are JS constants in the logic (`MODULES`, `LESSON_SETS`, `PRACTICE`, cases). Edit the text there.
- `SIM_SCEN` + `SIM_STAGES` are the simulator scenarios (Cases screen → "Take the ventilator"). Each stage is a `CASES` entry with `scen`/`stage`/`keep` (keep = carry the learner's settings over), its own `PATIENTS` physiology (`Cfn` = PEEP-dependent compliance, `autoBase` = baseline auto-PEEP, `fight` = asynchrony dips, `defs` = knob ranges), an `event` paragraph, a `brief`, bedside `actions` ([id, label, fb, optional `patient` switch, `needs` prerequisite, `bad`]), a `gas(m, acts, s, m0, c)` hook returning `{o2, co2}` multipliers, optional `vitals()`, and `goals(m, acts, s)`; the last stage carries a `debrief`. Action state lives in `state.acts[caseId]`.
- **Home NIV in the simulator.** Modes `NIV-CPAP`, `NIV-S`, `NIV-ST`, `NIV-T`, `iVAPS` (`NIV_MODES`, knobs `cpap`, `ipap`, `epap`, `vtt`, `ipmax`; `nivMap` turns them into PS or PC breaths). A stage with `niv: true` makes the setup wizard and the Modes menu offer only these. The child's breathing belongs to the patient: `eff` (effort), `rrOwn` (own rate), `trig: false` (cannot trigger), `Rfn(peep)` (upper-airway resistance that falls with EPAP/CPAP), `leak` (fraction of VT lost). Sim scenarios 03–07 (OSA, Duchenne, SMA, GBS, BPD) use them.
- **Stage extras.** `illus` (scene illustration in `assets/illus/*.jpg`; `STAGE_ILLUS` and `SC_ILLUS` map the older stages and the case discussions), `set` (ventilator settings applied when a `keep` stage opens: the team changed something before you arrived), `task1` (setup wording when the child is not yet on the ventilator), and `live` ({q, opts: [label, 1 if right, the patient's response]}: the orders the talk deck and the Live session show). A patient may carry `gap` (EtCO₂ to PaCO₂ gap). Sim scenarios 08–12 (Maya, Leo, the congenital heart, the 4-year-old boy, Suresh) come from the CP interactions ventilator script. Sim scenarios 13–15 (Anaya bronchiolitis, Ishaan pneumonia on bilevel, Zara extubated onto bilevel) are the acute NIV cases; their deck screens live in `tools/nivdata.py` and `tools/build_niv.py` builds `talks/niv.html`, `niv-cases.html`, `niv-ltv.html` (eight cases, room NLV) and `hfnc-niv-ltv.html` (the whole of Module 09 as one talk, room HNL) (theory helpers in `tools/talkkit.py`).
- **Sim scenarios 16–20** are the five original bedside cases (Case 01–05, ids `ards`, `asthma`, `postop`, `bronch1`, `bronch2`): each original case is stage 1, upgraded in place (scen/stage/t/brief/event), followed by two new `keep` stages (`c16b`…`c20c`). No plain cases remain on the Cases screen.
- **Sim scenarios 21–25** are the five Read-the-wave cases (`WAVES[0..4]`; stage 1 takes the wave case's story, assess and film via `WV(i)`), two stages each (`w21a`…`w25b`). Wave patients use `starve` (scooped pressure, volume control only), `saw` (sawtooth flow: secretions or circuit water) and `leakInv` (fraction lost around an uncuffed tube: VTe < VTi, volume trace does not return to zero), added in `model0`. The Read-the-wave screen itself is unchanged.
- **Scenario book PDF.** `tools/scenario_book/`: `node extract.js <repo> scen.json` reads every sim scenario from `index.html`, `node build.js <repo>` writes `book.html`, `node pdf.js` prints `VentCourse Simulator Scenarios.pdf` (run all three from that folder after changing scenarios).
- `SCENARIOS` holds the same two cases as question-and-answer walkthroughs ("Discuss the decisions", screen `scen`); `talks/difficult-scenarios.html` is generated from them. One patient, several stages; each stage has the bedside picture (`vit`, `exam`, `vent`, `abg`, optional `img`, `table`, `trace`), one question (`q`, `opts` = [label, 1 if right, feedback]), a `teach` take-away and a `lesson` link [module, index]. The last stage is a `debrief` list. Answers are saved with lesson progress (`sc`).
- **Simulation format (OPENPediatrics style).** Every case opens with only the case story and a drawing of the patient (`patient-figure.js`, `<patient-figure age="infant|toddler|child|teen" support="ett|bag|mask|niv|nc|trach|prone">`; age comes from the title via `AGE_BAND`, airway per stage: sim stages from `fig`/ventOn/prone, discussions from `SC_FIG`). Everything else (exam, X-ray, gas, handover, ventilator, monitor, chart) is asked for with chips. In sim stages the stage `story` is the cue; every stage `event` is held back until the last stage is solved, then shown together as "What was going on · stage by stage"; `brief` sits behind "Call a senior".
- **Free flow, no question-and-answer.** Case discussions (`SCENARIOS`) and wave cases (`WAVES`) show the `opts`/`fixes` as orders, shuffled per stage, given in any order; each order's feedback text is the patient's response, logged in the case log (`state.scLog[scenarioId]`, `state.wLog[i]`). The right order moves the case to the next stage in one continuous page (earlier stages stay above as "case so far"). `q` is no longer shown; `teach`/`teachList`/`lesson` appear only at the debrief, stage by stage. Wave cases reveal the right `names` entry as "What was going on" once fixed.
- **Vitals** render as a bedside monitor (`bedside-monitor.js`, `<bedside-monitor vitals='[["HR","145"],...]'>`): HR/SpO₂/RR as moving traces with big numbers, everything else as coloured tiles.
- **Performance.** `state.perf[key]` (key = case id, `w<i>` for wave cases, `<scenario>:<stage>` for discussions) logs open time, what was asked for and when (`req`), first action (`act`), senior call (`hint`), harmful orders (`bad`), checks and finish time. `perfReport()` turns it into the scored card (time, assessed before acting, information gathered, targets/decisions, senior, harmful orders).
- `support.js` is the runtime. Never edit it.
- `vent-graphics.js` draws all waveforms (`<vent-trace scenario="…">`).
- Talks in `talks/` use the same format; each slide is a `<section data-label>`.

## Design rules (Solstice, `_ds/`)
- Paper `#F3EDE3`, ink `#1B1A17`, single accent ember `#B04428`. Use `var(--…)` tokens.
- Instrument Serif headlines (one italic word), Hanken Grotesk body, IBM Plex Mono uppercase labels.
- Sentence case. No emoji. Lucide icons only.
- Mobile-first at 390px; tap targets ≥ 44px.

## Rules
- Clinical text is final. Don't reword it unless asked.
- Small, targeted edits. Don't restyle things you weren't asked to touch.
- After a change, run `npm start` and check `http://localhost:3000` at phone width.
- Commit with a plain-English message and push to `main` (Railway auto-deploys).
- Never commit passwords or keys beyond what's already in the files.

## Accounts API (`server.js`)
POST JSON: `/api/exists`, `/api/register`, `/api/login`, `/api/touch`, `/api/admin/list`, `/api/admin/role`, `/api/admin/delete`.
`/api/progress` (POST `{u, ph, prog?}`) saves and returns the user's lesson progress; the app also keeps a copy in localStorage (`vc-progress-<user>`). A lesson counts as done when its quiz is answered; Module 02 is done when its quiz is answered.
Nine modules, 84 lessons; Module 07 (Disease-specific ventilation) lives in `LESSON_SETS['07']`, Module 08 (Cardiopulmonary interactions) in `LESSON_SETS['08']`, Module 09 (High-flow, NIV and long-term ventilation, 31 lessons) in `LESSON_SETS['09']`: since 29 Oct it is built from the old Module 04 NIV lessons plus two BPICC lessons, followed by the 13 long-term lessons (the IIFE just above `MODULES`); Module 04 keeps the three indications lessons. Saved progress from before the merge (no `mg` flag) is remapped by `MIG09`, and progress saved with `mg: 1` (before the five interface, machine, RAM cannula, nebulisation and mouth-care lessons were added) by `MIG09B`, and progress saved with `mg: 2` (before the humidity and weaning, circuits and masks, ICEMAN and hourly chart lessons of 30 Oct, which cover the BPICC NIV chapter and the NIV station objectives) by `MIG09C`, all in `applyProg`; `progOf` now writes `mg: 3`. A lesson may carry `fig` + `figCap` (an image under `assets/`, shown above the video slot); the NIV figures are native SVG drawn by `tools/nivfigs.py` (one drawing, two palettes: `assets/niv/*.svg` for the app, inline with CSS tokens for the talks). The lesson count also appears as text in `index.html` (home card, Learn header, admin list): update all three when adding lessons. A lesson may carry `vidCap` + `vidNote` (renders a video slot) and `vid` (path to an mp4 under `assets/`; until it is set the slot shows a placeholder).
Username 2–40 chars (trimmed, lower-cased), password ≥ 6. Admin re-seeded from `ADMIN_PASSWORD` on boot.
EmailJS sends the sign-up code (service `service_po89bsd`, template `template_ndn664u`).

## Live sessions
A talk deck runs a live room: `/api/live/create|set|state|answer|results` (saved to `rooms.json` next to `users.json`, so a restart keeps the room and its answers; CORS open so the Present bundle can call Railway). Opening a fixed-code deck again returns the same room and key, never a reset. The deck re-sends the current question every 3 s and reconnects by itself; only the copy the speaker drives (a key press or click) moves the phones. The badge and the Join slide (`data-live-status`) show green with the phone count when the room is open. Phones that join before the deck opens wait and connect by themselves. `/present.html` lists the talk links for the speaker (live rooms run only from these web links or the Present bundles, never from a PDF or Google Slides). The app's "Live session" screen (`#live-CP1` or `#live-1234` joins directly) shows the case stage and question the speaker is on; answers appear on the deck. Case text and questions live in the `LIVE` const (between `// LIVE-START` and `// LIVE-END`), generated by `tools/build_decks.py` (entries: illustration, monitor, ventilator, orders; `<stage>_r` entries reveal each order with the patient's response). A deck can ask for a fixed room code (`<meta name="vc-live-room" content="CP1">`), so a printed QR code (`/#live-CP1`) always lands in its room; candidates join as guests.
**Candidates set the support.** A stage in `tools/deckdata.py` with `setq` ({ifaces, start}) gives the phone a settings panel (face mask, high-flow, NIV, ventilator; `LV_*` in the logic); answers go to `<stage>~s`. The order slide counts settings sent; the response slide reveals the answer settings and the room's median settings (`data-live-setq`). Case slides carry history only (`R2` / `LTV_R2` overrides: `head`, `hist`, `scr`, `after`); POCUS clips drop into `assets/pocus/<name>.mp4` (maya-ivc, maya-heart, leo-heart, leo-ivc, suresh-heart, suresh-ivc). `talks/cp-complete.html` (room CPF) is Part 1 + Part 2 with more theory after each stage (`FULL_TH`, `FOUNDATIONS` in the builder) and a POCUS normal-views slide. Theory slides not woven into the cases sit hidden (`data-deck-skip`) at the end of each deck.
**Every live slide.** `shell()` puts a join chip (QR + code) at the bottom of every visible slide, and gives every slide without a question a phone entry (`x<code><n>`: its title and key points). Phone orders use the slide's short wording; the phone ventilator draws the waveform (`vent.wave`).
**Branching cases (`talks/ards-asthma.html`, room ARDS, `tools/build_aa.py` + `tools/aadata.py`).** Each stage: slide (set + order) → the room (`data-live-mode="cloud"`: word cloud of settings, support percentages, severity of the room's patients from orders and settings) → the response. Orders carry a severity (0 better, 1 no change, 2 worse, 3 life-threatening) and settings are graded against safe ranges (`rule`); the same grading runs on the phone (`vcGrade`, `vcAdjMon` in the logic) and the deck (`tools/grade.js`). Each phone shows its own patient (response, reasons, adjusted monitor), a banner at the next stage from its last decision (`prev`), and its course at the end (`course`); the deck shows the room's course. Grades are drafts for the course director to review. **Each phone's own patient:** stages carry `phys` (lung C and R, weight, kind, and an anchor: the script's support, monitor and gas for that stage); the phone runs the candidate's sent settings (carried to later stages until changed) through the waveform engine and a small physiology (`lvSupport`, `lvPatient` in the logic: CO₂ from alveolar ventilation, oxygen from FiO₂ and mean airway pressure, BP from mean airway pressure in ARDS and auto-PEEP in asthma, plus the order and settings grade), so its ventilator, waves, measured values, alarms and monitor are its own. Since 30 Oct the physiology is `physio.js` (`window.VCPhysio`): a two-compartment lung (open baby lung + recruitable lung, both able to overstretch), shunt and dead-space gas exchange with mixed venous O₂, and a heart–lung circulation (venous return, RV afterload, baroreflex), calibrated per stage to the script (`calibrate`), run per candidate (`run`); the ARDS constants were fitted to case 1's PEEP titration sheet. `node tools/physio_test.js` checks it against textbook behaviour and the scripts (44 checks; keep them passing). After a change the phone's numbers settle (O₂ ~25 s, CO₂ ~60 s, accelerated) via `lvSettle`. Faculty review: `tools/physio_review.js` prints the scenarios on the expert scoring sheet. Knob changes preview the waves before sending. **Typed-order version:** `WORDS=1 python3 tools/build_aa.py` builds `talks/ards-asthma-words.html` (room ARDSW, phone keys prefixed `w`): phones type the order; `tools/match.js` (also copied into the logic between `VC-MATCH-START/END`) reads each answer as an option or Other, and the room slide shows the word cloud and the options as percentages.

**Live simulation (`sim.html`, data `sim-data.js` from `python3 tools/build_sim.py`).** A facilitator-run simulation of the same two cases, no illustrations. One room, three screens chosen by the link: `sim.html#control-ROOM` (facilitator: case text to read, exam, the script's numbers, the expected action and what every choice does, stage buttons, orders the team gives, events to trigger, investigations to release, log and debrief with CSV), `#vent-ROOM` (the ventilator, or oxygen / high-flow / NIV when the stage allows: modes, knobs with Confirm, waveforms, measured values, inspiratory and expiratory holds, alarms) and `#monitor-ROOM` (full-screen patient monitor: ECG, pleth, capnography, NIBP, alarms and tones; released results — gas, X-ray, POCUS clips, lung ultrasound clips (from the user's `pocus` repo assets, transcoded: `lung-ards-anterior/lateral/posterior.mp4` for ARDS, `lung-asthma-sliding.mp4` for asthma; after a pneumothorax event `lung-ptx-absent-sliding.mp4` + `lung-ptx-barcode.mp4`; IVC shows `ivc-collapsing.mp4` when under-filled and `ivc-plethoric.mp4` after ≥30 mL/kg fluid — set in `POCUS`/`EVENT_POCUS` in `tools/build_sim.py`), titration sheet — appear on its right). Server: `/api/sim/create|get|act` (rooms in `sims.json`); every change is stored with the server's time in `hist`, and each screen replays it through `physio.js` (calibrated per stage), so all screens show the same patient. Numbers settle with time constants (O₂ ~30 s, CO₂ ~90 s, circulation ~10 s); a stage jump starts from the stage's own numbers, and a stage that ended worse or life-threatening carries a sicker patient into the next one (`debt`). Orders and events act through the physiology (fluids raise venous return, bronchodilators lower resistance, prone and iNO cut shunt, pneumothorax stiffens the lung and drops venous return, a plug raises resistance, disconnection or a dislodged tube stops ventilation).
