Present with live phones

  1. Open the talk below on the laptop, in Chrome. Not a PDF, not Google Slides: only these links run the live room.
  2. Press F for full screen, then the arrow keys (or the clicker).
  3. On the Join slide, check the green line: "Room … is open · N phones connected". Red means no internet on the laptop.
  4. Candidates scan the QR code. Phones that join early wait, and connect by themselves when you open the talk.
