Debrief

2-year-old girl · 10 kg
1NIV is a trial with a stop rule. Write the failure criteria down before you start.
2Intubation in ARDS is a resuscitation: fluids, pressor and a cuffed tube ready.
3Any mode works. The limits are what matter: VT 6–8 mL/kg, plateau ≤ 28–30, driving pressure ≤ 15.
4Oxygenation = FiO₂ + mean airway pressure. PEEP first, then inspiratory time, both checked against the flow scalar.
5Titrate PEEP to best compliance or lowest driving pressure, and watch the blood pressure.
6Asynchrony: check the tube, secretions and chest first, then sedation, then the briefest paralysis.
7Outside the ventilator: fluids, infection, haemoglobin, haemodynamics, abdomen, chest wall.
8Rescue ladder: prone → HFOV → ECMO discussion. Accept SpO₂ 88–92% and a high CO₂ with pH ≥ 7.20.
