Why

Leo: what now?

Answer
Why

A dilated RV, a flattened septum and a fixed IVC is a right heart failing against its load. Fluid pushes the septum further into the LV. Reduce mean airway pressure, correct hypoxia and acidosis, start inhaled nitric oxide, hold the systemic pressure with adrenaline, accept SpO₂ 88–90% and pH above 7.20, and make the ECMO call now.

Maya, minute 12: HR 80, BP 40/20

Answer
Why

The circulation has lost its drive and its preload at the same moment. Replace the drive with adrenaline, give the venous return back with slow breaths and fluid, and check the tube. Pressure and sedation make it worse; atropine does not fill an empty, unloaded heart.

Why
