Why

Answer
Why

A dilated RV, a flattened septum and a fixed IVC is a right heart failing against its load. Fluid pushes the septum further into the LV. Maya, minute 12: HR 80, BP 40/20

Answer
Why

The circulation has lost its drive and its preload at the same moment. Replace the drive with adrenaline, give the venous return back with slow breaths and fluid, and check the tube. Pressure and sedation make it worse; atropine does not fill an empty, unloaded heart.
