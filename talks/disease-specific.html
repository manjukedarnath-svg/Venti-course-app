ARDS: the stiff lung

PARDS: the PALICC-2 definition

Definition · 2023
Age: from term birth to young adulthood
Timing: within 7 days of a known insult
Imaging: new opacities, not explained by atelectasis or effusion
Origin: not primarily cardiac failure or fluid overload
Oxygenation: OI ≥ 4 or OSI ≥ 5 invasive; S/F ≤ 250 on NIV
PALICC-2, Pediatr Crit Care Med 2023

Is this lung recruitable? The R/I ratio

Recruitment-to-inflation · method
Airway opening pressure first: slow inflation from PEEP 5
Compliance at low PEEP: VT ÷ (Pplat − PEEP)
Ventilate at PEEP 15, drop to 5 in one breath
Released − inflation (Clow × ΔPEEP) = recruited volume
R/I = (recruited ÷ ΔPEEP) ÷ Clow
Chen et al, AJRCCM 2020 · passive patient, no leak, volume control, same VT
