AIt rises
BIt falls from 9 to 6
CIt is unchanged
DOnly oxygenation changes

Raise the EPAP

Answer
Why

Support is IPAP − EPAP. On a home bilevel device, raising EPAP alone lowers the support. Raise IPAP with it to keep the same help.
